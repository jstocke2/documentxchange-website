Frequently asked questions

Start here for answers to the most common DocumentXchange questions.

What is DocumentXchange?

DocumentXchange is a marketplace where users can request, contribute, search for, purchase, and access public-record documents and related metadata. DocumentXchange is not a government agency, law firm, title company, or official records custodian.

How do I request a document?

In the U.S. mobile app, open the Request tab, select the document type and location, and add any identifying details that can help locate the correct record. DocumentXchange estimates the page count and applicable purchase price automatically. Posting is free, and you pay only if you choose to buy a document submitted for the request. Requests remain open for 14 days.

How do I contribute a document?

In the U.S. mobile app, eligible U.S.-based contributors can select Contribute, photograph every page clearly, keep every line, signature, seal, page number, and other meaningful detail visible, provide accurate document details, and review the automated quality results. Showing the physical page edges is preferred, but a tight intact scan is acceptable when nothing is clipped. You must have the legal right to distribute every page.

How does the requester promotion work?

If the offer appears in your account, you may explicitly apply it when posting an eligible request or leave it off for later. Requests are always free to post. Each requester may claim one offer per campaign. Once you submit an opted-in request, the offer is claimed even if that request is later cancelled or expires. Only the requester who posted that request can receive the promotional price, and only when buying the approved document that fulfills it. Checkout shows the exact price, the contributor's normal target payout is unchanged, and DocumentXchange keeps $0; external Apple/Google/Stripe/tax/refund/payout costs may apply.

Must contributors be based in the United States?

Yes. Uploading documents, publishing listings, and fulfilling requests are limited to U.S.-based contributors. The website supports browsing, purchasing, saved searches, libraries, and account management, but it does not accept new requests, uploads, or request fulfillment. New requests must be submitted through the U.S. mobile app.

This restriction is enforced by the DocumentXchange backend across the website, iPhone app, and Android app. Before accepting an upload, DocumentXchange verifies that the contributor's Stripe account is a U.S. account, required identity and address details were submitted, and payouts are enabled. When a reliable network-country signal is available, an explicitly non-U.S. upload location is also blocked. Complete or update verification under Profile → Payment Methods.

How does document scanning work?

DocumentXchange may use OCR and artificial intelligence to extract searchable information, detect possible duplicates, classify records, and identify quality problems. Each page is also checked for clipped text, signatures, seals, page numbers, tables, and other content at its image boundaries. High-confidence cropped pages are rejected; uncertain cases remain private for administrator review. Automated results can be inaccurate, so contributors must still review every page and field.

The upload normally finishes in about 10–20 seconds. OCR and full document validation continue privately in the background and may take up to 5 minutes. The document is not available to buyers while these checks are running. DocumentXchange notifies the contributor when the document is approved or needs attention.

How do contributor royalties work?

Eligible contributors receive the royalty percentage displayed in the app when uploading or fulfilling a request. Royalties may be adjusted for refunds, chargebacks, duplicate or unlawful content, fraud, and payment reversals.

Where can I see documents I purchased?

Sign in and open Library. Purchased documents should appear under your document purchases. If a completed purchase is missing, contact support with the purchase date and document title.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases made through Apple are subject to Apple’s refund process and policies. For missing, inaccessible, unlawfully posted, or materially misdescribed content, contact DocumentXchange support so we can investigate.

How do I report an inappropriate document?

Use the report option available on the document or page. Include the affected page numbers and a clear explanation. For urgent privacy, safety, or legal concerns, email support directly.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile, select Delete Account, and confirm the request. Transaction, payout, fraud-prevention, dispute, and legal-compliance records may be retained when required or permitted by law.

Is DocumentXchange an official records source?

No. Documents are contributed by users. Verify important records directly with the appropriate court, county recorder, government office, or other official custodian before relying on them.

Account deletion

  1. Open DocumentXchange and sign in.
  2. Open the Profile tab.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation notice and confirm deletion.

If you cannot access the app, email documentxchangesupport@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account.

Account deletion removes or de-identifies account data and contributed content from active systems, subject to reasonable processing time and legally required retention. Copies already purchased or downloaded by other users cannot necessarily be removed from their possession.
